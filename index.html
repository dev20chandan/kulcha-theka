Our Story

The Taste of Amritsar, Across North India

Kulcha Theka is a pure vegetarian restaurant devoted to one craft — the perfect Amritsari stuffed Kulcha. Crisp on the outside, generously stuffed inside, roasted fresh in the tandoor and finished with a slab of melting butter. Served alongside our slow-cooked Chole, fluffy Bhature and crispy Tikkis.

Every plate is prepared fresh to order — no shortcuts, no compromise. Just honest Punjabi food the whole family can enjoy, at prices that keep you coming back.
